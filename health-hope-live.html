A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we meet.

Is it really free?

Yes. Health & Hope Live is free to attend. No payment or membership is required.

Can I come back another week?

Absolutely. Register for each session you’d like to attend so we can send you the right confirmation and updates.

What time is it where I live?

The regular event starts at 6:30 PM in America/New_York. The registration menu also shows your local time. Eastern daylight saving changes are taken into account.

Where is my Zoom link?

Joining information is shared privately with confirmed registrants. Please check your confirmation email, including your spam folder. Contact ReVitalized Academy if you need help.

How is my information used?

We use your name and email to manage your selected event, send joining details and event updates, and keep registration and attendance history. Event records are separate from private health assessments. Optional marketing preferences are recorded separately. Contact contact@revitalizedacademy.com for privacy questions or to change your communication preferences.

What if a session changes?

Only available sessions appear in the registration menu. If a session is canceled or rescheduled, registrants will receive an event update after the change is confirmed.
