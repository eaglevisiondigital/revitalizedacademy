J

Justyn Oliver

Co-Founder

Justyn’s interest in health and wellness began early, inspired by watching his father help people through the family gym in British Columbia, Canada. By his mid-teens, he was already working in the health and fitness industry and learning firsthand how movement, nutrition, mindset and consistent habits can shape a person’s life.

His story also includes significant personal adversity. Those experiences shaped his passion for helping people find clarity, build resilience and create a life they are excited to live. Today, he brings that combination of practical experience, coaching, education and personal transformation into the ReVitalized Academy framework.

Justyn’s work centers on helping people understand the bigger picture of their health and turn that knowledge into a personalized, sustainable lifestyle.

E

Elle Oliver

Co-Founder

Elle has worked alongside Justyn for years, bringing her own journey, perspective and passion for whole-person wellness into ReVitalized Academy. Her background includes years in the modeling industry and personal experiences that shaped how she thinks about health, confidence, identity and the pressure people can feel around appearance.

Those experiences helped create a deep desire to help others recognize their value beyond appearances and outside opinions. Elle’s perspective adds a powerful relational and practical dimension to the ReVitalized approach - helping people pursue health in a way that supports confidence, purpose and a sustainable family lifestyle.

Together, Justyn and Elle credit their family, their faith and their commitment to helping others as central to the mission behind ReVitalized Academy.
