Frequently Asked Questions

How long has ReVitalized Academy been in business for?

ReVitalized Academy is built on years of personal experience, client transformation, study and practical application. Justyn and Elle have been helping people through holistic health principles for years, and the Academy is the structured vehicle that brings that guidance, coaching and community together in one place.

Are your services expensive?

Compared with trying to piece together separate coaching, training, nutrition support, accountability tools and education on your own, ReVitalized Academy is designed to deliver far more value at a much lower overall cost. The goal is not to create more financial pressure—it is to give people a clear, sustainable path that makes health simpler and more practical.

Who do you take on as a client?

ReVitalized Academy is best for people and families who want real guidance, are open to a holistic approach, and are willing to learn, apply and stay consistent. It is especially helpful for those who feel overwhelmed, stuck, or frustrated by one-size-fits-all advice and want a clearer plan built around their life.

Where are you located?

ReVitalized Academy serves clients remotely, which allows people to work with the team from many locations. The experience is designed to be accessible online through coaching, courses, community and digital support tools.

How do I work with ReVitalized Academy?

The best next step is to start with the application or request a free health assessment. From there, the team can help determine the best path based on your goals, starting point and current challenges.

Request a free health assessment →

What is the Longevity Matrix?

The Longevity Matrix is ReVitalized Academy’s visual framework for helping people build a complete lifestyle around four key areas: Energized, Athletic, Refined and Organized. Each focus area contains practical principles and tools that work together to help you feel better, move better, look better and live healthier with more consistency.

Why do you recommend ionized hydrogen water so strongly?

It is recommended because hydration quality matters, and the ReVitalized team sees hydrogen water as one of the supportive tools that can help people improve daily hydration, recovery and cellular support. It is not positioned as a magic answer, but as one potentially helpful tool within a broader lifestyle system.

Who do you recommend ActivePure purifiers for?

ActivePure purifiers may be a strong fit for individuals and families who want cleaner air and cleaner surfaces in their home, particularly those who are focused on overall wellness, environmental load reduction, or creating a healthier home environment.

Why do you recommend nano silver products?

Nano silver products are recommended selectively as part of a broader wellness toolkit. ReVitalized Academy highlights them because they may offer supportive benefits in the right context, but the team’s larger emphasis is always on education, lifestyle application and helping clients understand why each tool is being used.

Is the coaching really personalized?

Yes. ReVitalized Academy is not built around a cookie-cutter system. Coaching is meant to help you apply the right priorities in the right order, with accountability and support that matches your goals and real-life situation.

Is ReFuel available now?

Not yet. ReFuel is currently being previewed as part of the future ReVitalized ecosystem and is planned for a later release. It is something exciting that is coming, but it is not something you need to wait for in order to begin your health journey now.

Do I need to wait for the app or future tools?

No. The coaching, education, accountability and community foundation already exist today. The app and future tools are meant to strengthen the experience—not delay it. You can begin now and grow into the expanded ecosystem as it is released.

Can families go through ReVitalized Academy together?

Yes. The ReVitalized philosophy is strongly family-oriented. The team wants health in the home to become simple, sustainable and something that can bless the entire family—not just one individual in isolation.
