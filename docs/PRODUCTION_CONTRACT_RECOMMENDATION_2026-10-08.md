## Approved business choices

1. Holistic Foundations Monthly should end after the six-month Initial Term unless the client affirmatively accepts a new renewal agreement. Do not automatically convert it to a paid month-to-month contract.
2. Holistic Foundations Pay in Full should expire after the 12-month Paid Term unless the client affirmatively accepts a new agreement. Do not renew automatically.
3. Accept cancellation or early-termination notice through either a portal request or written email. Do not require both. Record the submission timestamp and provide a durable confirmation.
4. Use a seven-calendar-day grace period after a failed scheduled payment before suspending access. Send notice promptly; reinstate access when the account is brought current.

These choices reduce surprise charges and preserve evidence of cancellation. They also align the product design with Québec's September 12, 2026 online-subscription requirement for an accessible online cancellation mechanism where applicable. They do not determine whether the agreement is legally classified as a physical-fitness-studio, course, or other service contract.

### 3. SERVICES, TERM, AND FEES.

The Client(s) will enroll in the Holistic Foundations program as described in Appendix A. The Initial Term is six consecutive months. The price is six monthly installments of CAD $89.00, for a total contractual price of CAD $534.00 during the Initial Term. No separate deposit is required.

The six-month Initial Term is a minimum payment commitment. A request to stop participating, stop using the Program, or terminate access during the Initial Term does not by itself eliminate the remaining installment obligations. This minimum commitment is subject to any cancellation, refund, or other consumer right that cannot lawfully be waived and to any written exception expressly approved by the Company.

This Agreement and the Client's access under it end automatically at the conclusion of the Initial Term. The Agreement does not renew automatically and does not convert to a paid month-to-month agreement. Continued participation after the Initial Term requires a new renewal offer accepted by the Client in writing. No renewal payment may be charged without the Client's separate acceptance and any separate payment authorization required by the approved payment process.

### 3.3 Payment Authorization.

This Agreement records the Client's payment obligation. It does not, by itself, authorize the Company to debit a bank account or charge a payment card. Any electronic or recurring payment authorization must be presented and accepted separately through the approved payment process.

### 4. REFUND, EARLY TERMINATION, AND ACCESS POLICY.

The following business rules apply subject to any cancellation, refund, cooling-off, or other consumer right that cannot lawfully be waived.

### 4.1 Final Sale After Access Is Granted.

Once Program and platform access has been granted, each payment already made is final and non-refundable merely because the Client stops using or chooses not to continue participating in the Program. Nothing in this section limits any cancellation, refund, or other remedy that applicable law does not permit the Client to waive. The Company may also approve an exception in writing.

### 4.2 Notice of Early Termination.

The Client may submit written notice through either the cancellation request available in the client portal or the designated support email address. Either method is sufficient; the Client is not required to use both. The Company will record the date and time the notice is received and provide confirmation through the portal or email.

Submitting notice during the Initial Term does not by itself eliminate the remaining minimum payment commitment. If applicable law gives the Client a right to terminate or limits the amount the Company may retain or collect, that law controls. If access remains paid and current after notice, access continues through the applicable paid service period unless the Client asks that access be closed earlier or the Company is permitted to suspend or terminate access under this Agreement.

### 4.3 Failed Payments and Seven-Day Grace Period.

If a scheduled installment is not successfully received on its due date, the Client has seven calendar days to bring the account current. The Company may provide notice by email and through the client portal. If the account remains past due after the seven-day grace period, the Company may suspend Program and platform access until the past-due amount is successfully paid.

A temporary suspension does not automatically cancel this Agreement, waive an amount lawfully due, extend the Initial Term, or create a refund right. Access will be restored after the account is brought current, subject to normal processing time and the Client's continued compliance with this Agreement.

### 4.4 End of Access.

Unless access is suspended or closed earlier under this Agreement or applicable law, access ends at the conclusion of the Initial Term. Ending access does not authorize any additional charge. Historical account information will be retained or removed according to the Company's approved privacy, records-retention, and legal obligations.

## Publication blocker for the CAD $960 one-payment model

The official Québec consumer office states that a physical-fitness-studio/fitness-or-weight-management contract generally cannot be collected in one installment when the total exceeds CAD $100 and services extend beyond three days. It also describes mandatory cancellation rights and refunds for services not rendered. Holistic Foundations includes fitness, weight-management, nutrition, and coaching features, so its exact legal classification must be confirmed before publishing a CAD $960 single-payment agreement or an unqualified all-sales-final clause.

Accordingly, the pay-in-full agreement must remain unpublished until qualified Canadian counsel confirms either:

1. Holistic Foundations is not subject to those installment and cancellation rules; or
2. the payment schedule and cancellation/refund text are revised to satisfy the applicable rules.

The same classification review should confirm whether the proposed six-month monthly minimum commitment and final-sale language require the prescribed Québec cancellation clause/form or other changes.
