# Holistic Foundations agreement structures v2 — held draft specification

Date: 2026-10-10

Status: structural preparation only. No agreement template, published revision, or active mapping is created by this package. `production_client_agreement_publication` must remain `held` and MK7 remains historical. Published MK8 is an editorial source only.

## Required future revisions

Each future revision must be separately reviewed, published, and mapped by exact `plan_code` and market/currency. No revision may fall back across plans.

### Monthly Flex

Plan code: `holistic_foundations_monthly_flex`

The future agreement must describe the approved 100-per-month flexible product, recurring monthly cadence, no multi-month minimum, cancellation/renewal rules approved for the applicable market, payment authorization, access suspension, refunds/non-waivable rights, and member/coaching obligations. It must not contain a three-month minimum or annual prepayment language.

### 3-Month Commitment

Plan code: `holistic_foundations_3_month`

The future agreement must describe 89 per month, a three-month minimum contractual amount of 267, month-to-month continuation after the initial commitment without a new automatic minimum term, market-approved cancellation/renewal rules, payment authorization, access suspension, refunds/non-waivable rights, and member/coaching obligations. MK8 may be used only as editorial source; it is not mapped by this package.

### Established Member Annual

Plan code: `holistic_foundations_annual_established`

The future agreement must describe one 948 annual payment, 12 months of coverage, at least six qualifying months of authoritative participation or an audited Owner/Admin override, affirmative client selection, no 79-per-month installment plan, no automatic migration, market-approved expiry/renewal/cancellation rules, payment authorization, access suspension, refunds/non-waivable rights, and member/coaching obligations.

## Required publication gate

A later, separately authorized legal-release migration must:

1. confirm qualified review for the exact market and currency;
2. create immutable plan-specific revisions;
3. publish only the reviewed revisions;
4. insert `program_agreement_requirements` rows with exact `plan_code` and currency;
5. verify there is no MK7 fallback and no cross-plan match;
6. release `production_client_agreement_publication` only in the same controlled transaction;
7. leave payment processing disabled until separate provider acceptance and live-charge approval.

This document intentionally contains no final legal drafting and makes no enforceability claim.
