Preparing for your first session

The questions your coach should already understand.

You should not have to start your story over.

Your coach should come into the first meeting having reviewed what you already shared. The first conversation can then focus on clarification, priorities and what needs to happen next—not repeating the entire intake.

  • How long has it been since you were at your healthiest?
  • What is your dream level of health?
  • Why do you want to get healthier, and who are you getting healthier for?
  • Why do you believe you have not achieved the health you want yet?
  • What are you most afraid may happen if things do not change?
  • Why do you believe ReVitalized may be the right program to help you?
